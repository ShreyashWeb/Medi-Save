Frequently Asked Questions

Facts regarding generic medicines, pricing regulations, and data safety.

Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) is an initiative by the Department of Pharmaceuticals, Government of India. It provides quality generic medicines with the exact same active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), potency, safety, and bio-equivalence as expensive branded medicines, manufactured under strict WHO-GMP compliance.
Branded medicines carry heavy commercial marketing costs, medical representative commissions, brand licensing fees, and retailer margins. Jan Aushadhi medicines are procured directly from certified manufacturers in bulk by the government and distributed without advertising overhead, passing 50% to 90% savings directly to patients.

Gemma 4 Open-Weights Architecture

MediSave integrates Gemma 4 (Google DeepMind open-weights model) running locally via Ollama with zero external cloud telemetry.

What Gemma 4 Does:

  • Reads visual text off blurred medicine strips and printed prescriptions.
  • Extracts trade names, strengths (e.g. 650mg), and forms.
  • Runs with temperature 0.0 and strict JSON schema output.

What Gemma 4 NEVER Does:

  • Never generates medical, clinical, or dosage advice.
  • Never invents, predicts, or interpolates drug prices.
  • Every displayed fact must trace to our verified government database.
Privacy Commitment: Prescription photos are processed in volatile RAM only and are immediately discarded. No images or patient details are ever written to disk or transmitted to third-party APIs.
