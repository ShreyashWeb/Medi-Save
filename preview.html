FAQs

Drug prices vary by pharmacy. Use BudgetRx to find current prices and discounts.

What is BudgetRx? How can it help me save?

BudgetRx is a free price comparison resource that provides significant savings to its users every month by finding them the lowest prescription prices at their local pharmacies. Visit budgetrx.net to access coupons that can help you save up to 80% on medications.

Is BudgetRx free?

Yes! BudgetRx is completely free for anyone to use. You do not need to register or sign up in order to use the BudgetRx coupons listed on BudgetRx.com.

Where can I use BudgetRx?

BudgetRx is accepted at over 60,000+ retail pharmacies in the United States, Puerto Rico, and the U.S. Virgin Islands — including major chains like CVS, Walgreens, Kroger, Rite Aid, Costco, Walmart and more!

Can I reuse my coupon? Do they expire?

BudgetRx coupons do not expire; you can use and reuse the coupons as often as you like. To make sure you get the best price, we recommend you have your pharmacy keep the card on file (similar to your insurance card). Next time you need a prescription, ask the pharmacy to give you the price with insurance or with this card and enjoy the savings!

How do I find a coupon?

Step 1: Search for your medication on the homepageTo locate a coupon for your medication, visit budgetrx.net, type in your drug’s name in the search field and click the "SAVE NOW" button
Step 2: Select the strength and quantity and the prices will automatically appear.
Step 3: Add your location

Once you provide the drug information, the pharmacies will automatically appear based on pricing from lowest to most expensive. If you do not recognize any of the pharmacies listed then you can enter your zipcode and hit search. This will update the pharmacy list based on location.
A BudgetRx coupon works just like any coupon you take into the grocery store. At the time of checkout, hand the coupon to your pharmacist and save off the retail price of your prescription.
When you go to the pharmacist to pick up your prescription, let them know that you have a coupon before they scan your medication to begin checkout.
Or, if you're bringing a paper prescription to the pharmacy, present the pharmacist with your BudgetRx coupon along with your prescription. This way, the BudgetRx coupon will be automatically applied when you pick up your medication!

Can I use BudgetRx if I have insurance?

Yes! Anyone can use BudgetRx, regardless of whether they have insurance or not—and this is true if you have commercial insurance, Medicare, Medicaid, or any other type of insurance.
To use BudgetRx, just let your pharmacy know that you’ll use a BudgetRx discount instead of your insurance when you pay for your prescription medication.
When using BudgetRx instead of your insurance, the amount you pay will not automatically be applied toward your deductible.

How can I use a coupon without printing?

1. Text or email a coupon to yourself
After searching for your prescription on the BudgetRx website, click on the green Get Free Coupon button
This will open your coupon in a new window. You'll see three buttons in the top left corner of the Coupon page that say: Print, Text and Email.

To Text the Coupon
Click the Text button in the top right-hand corner of the coupon page.
A box will appear where you can enter your cell phone number to have the coupon sent directly to your phone. Enter your cell phone number into the box, and click the SEND button.
This will send a text with a link to a mobile webpage containing the coupon (show this page to your pharmacist to receive the discount).

To Email the Coupon
Click the Email button in the top left-hand corner of the coupon print page.
A box will appear at the top of the coupon print page where you can enter an email address to have the coupon sent directly to your email. Enter an email address into the box and click the SEND button.
If you would like to receive promotional emails from BudgetRx, check the box that says Send me savings offers and prescription alerts. If you do not wish to receive any additional emails from BudgetRx, uncheck this box before clicking the SEND button.

2. You can request a discount card in the mail
While we recommend using the coupons for greater savings, if you're unable to use the coupons from any of the options listed above, you can submit a request for a free BudgetRx Pharmacy Discount Card. Once requested, we'll mail you a card and it should arrive within 7-10 business days.

What if I am having an issue at the pharmacy?

The prices listed on BudgetRx are updated frequently and are generally very accurate, but if you have experienced an issue with a price at your pharmacy, please give us a call at 1800-180-8080 (Jan Aushadhi Helpline). Our BudgetRx support line is always happy to help, and can typically resolve most issues while you’re in the pharmacy.

GENERIC MEDICINE ESSENTIALS

Everything You Need to Know About Jan Aushadhi Generics

Regulated under the Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP), generic medicines provide the exact same therapeutic outcome at 50% to 90% lower prices compared to heavily marketed brands.

Same Molecule & Strength

A generic drug contains the identical active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) in the same dosage form, route of administration, and safety profile as the brand-name medicine.

WHO-GMP & NABL Tested

Manufactured strictly in WHO-GMP certified facilities. Every single batch is independently tested and verified at NABL-accredited government laboratories before release.

Regulated Fair Pricing

Prices are fixed by the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA). The massive savings come from eliminating multi-crore marketing campaigns and retail brand royalties.
